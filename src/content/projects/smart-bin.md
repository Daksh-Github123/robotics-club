## The problem

Half of what went into our recycling bins wasn't recyclable.

## How it works

Hold an item up to the camera and a small on-device model lights up the right slot. Load cells weigh each bin so we can show weekly recycling stats on a screen in the cafeteria.

## What we learned

Training data from our own cafeteria beat any dataset we downloaded.
