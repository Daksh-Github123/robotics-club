## The problem

The lost-and-found bin was one giant pile. Nobody could find anything, so nothing got claimed.

## How it works

Items go on a small conveyor. A color sensor classifies each item, and a servo-driven flap pushes it into the right bin: clothes, bottles, electronics, everything else.

## What we learned

- Belts slip. A lot. Tension matters.
- Color sensors hate fluorescent lighting.
- Cable management is a skill, not an afterthought.
