## The problem

Things get left behind every day: in a classroom, on the field, on the bus. Finding them means a trip to the Lost & Found desk and hoping your item is there.

## How it works

1. **Found items get logged.** Staff collect items, or finders hand them in, and each one is photographed and logged the day it arrives.
2. **Search the lost and found.** Students describe what they lost (colour, brand, where they last had it), and the search, with a little help from AI, shows the closest matches.
3. **Claim it, then collect.** When you claim an item, Reunited emails you a code, also shown on your dashboard. You show that code at the Lost & Found desk to collect it.

Students sign in with Google or an emailed magic link to search, claim and track their items.

## Where it's used

Reunited is live at [reunited.co.in](https://www.reunited.co.in/) for Inventure Academy.
