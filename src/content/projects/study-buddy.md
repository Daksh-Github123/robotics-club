## What it is

Study Buddy connects students who want help with a subject to students who can help them. When you sign in, you choose whether you want to be a **mentee** or a **mentor**.

## Where it's used

Study Buddy is already running at the school's own web address, [studybuddy.inventureacademy.com](https://studybuddy.inventureacademy.com/).

## How it works

Students sign in with their Google account, pick their role and get started.
