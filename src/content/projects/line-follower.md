## About Sprint 1

In the club's first sprint, Robotics Club ran as a robotics course. Daksh Singh taught the basics of robotics, and every member built the same project: a line follower.

## How it works

Infrared sensors under the robot detect a dark line on a light floor. The microcontroller reads the sensors and steers the motors to keep the robot on the line.

## What changed after

From Sprint 2 onward, the club became project-first: each student chooses their own project, and the teaching covers whatever that project needs.
